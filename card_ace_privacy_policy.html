CARD ACE: SA CARD GAMES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your email, phone number or ID number.
  • Player names you type, your settings and your game setups stay on your phone.
  • All games, including the computer players and hints, run on your phone.
  • Data leaves your phone only through Google's advertising and Google Play's billing, and, when you play with friends over Wi-Fi, directly to their phones.
  • We don't sell your data or keep a profile of you.

Who we are

Card Ace: SA Card Games ("Card Ace", "the app", "we", "us") is published by Ekspeace. Contact us at Ekspeace@gmail.com.

Card Ace teaches and plays traditional card games. It involves no betting or real money, and nothing in the app can be won or exchanged for money.

Advertising

Card Ace is free and funded by ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device information and your advertising ID to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and an "Advert privacy options" entry in the app's settings lets you change your choice.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings under Google > Ads.

Ads are limited to a teen content rating. A banner appears on the home screen and an occasional full-screen ad appears between rounds, never in the middle of a game. If you buy Card Ace Pro, all ads are switched off.

Purchases

The once-off "Card Ace Pro" purchase is made through Google Play's billing system under Google's own terms and privacy policy. It is tied to the Google account on your phone, so it is restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers for this app and hold no database of players. Games with friends connect phone to phone on your local network.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. To delete it, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data held by Google (advertising and purchases) is managed in your Google account.

Children

Card Ace is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

In South Africa the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you the right to know what personal information is held about you and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. If you have a concern, contact us first at Ekspeace@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date at the top.
