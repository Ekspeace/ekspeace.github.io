Inspecta Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

In short

  • Your inspections, photos, signatures and reports stay on your phone. You don't need an account to use Inspecta.
  • Reading text from a photo with the camera happens on your phone. The photo is not sent anywhere.
  • Backups to Google Drive go to your own Drive, in a private folder only Inspecta can use.
  • AI photo reading is optional. When you use it, the photo you choose is sent to be read and is not kept by us.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Inspecta Pro has no ads.
  • We never sell your information.

Who we are

Inspecta is an app made by Ekspeace in South Africa ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Inspecta app for Android handles, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and Google Play's requirements.

Questions or requests: ekspeace@gmail.com. Our Information Officer can be reached at the same address.

Information that stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Inspecta is saved only in Inspecta's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • your name, company, phone number and logo;
  • inspections, including addresses or site names, notes, answers and scores;
  • photos you take or choose, and signatures;
  • templates you build, streaks, badges and settings.

We do not receive or see any of this. If you uninstall Inspecta, Android deletes it, so please make a backup first.

When you share a PDF report, Inspecta hands it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or email. What happens next is up to that app and the person you send it to. Reports can contain the names, addresses, photos and signatures you added, so please share them only with people who should see them.

Reading text from photos

The camera's text reading (for example for number plates, serial numbers or meter readings) uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. The photo is not sent to us or to Google for this.

Backups

Backup files

A backup file contains your inspections, templates, details, logo, signatures and, if you choose, your photos. It is not protected by a password, so keep it somewhere safe.

Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Inspecta asks Google for permission to use only its own hidden app folder in your Drive. Inspecta cannot see, open or change any of your other files. Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Inspecta so the app can show which account is connected. The backups belong to you, live in your Google account and are covered by Google's privacy policy. We cannot access them.

To remove them, open Google Drive on a computer, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Inspecta and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can disconnect Drive in Inspecta at any time.

AI photo reading (optional)

AI photo reading only runs when you tap its button on a photo. It sends that photo, made smaller first, and the question being asked (such as "describe the defect") over an encrypted connection to Inspecta's server, run on Google Firebase, and from there to OpenAI, which reads it and sends the result back to your phone. Neither our server nor we keep the photo or the result. OpenAI processes it under its terms for business use of its API, which state that this information is not used to train its models by default and may be kept for a limited time to prevent abuse.

AI photo reading uses credits. To keep your credit balance safe across phones, you sign in with Google before using it. Our server stores your Google account ID, your credit balance and when credits were added or used. It does not store your photos.

Please do not photograph information you are not allowed to share with a service provider.

Advertising

The free version of Inspecta shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, device type and how you interact with ads, and may use it to show ads that suit your interests. Where the law requires it, Inspecta asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in Settings. This is covered by Google's advertising policies.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings. Inspecta Pro removes ads.

Payments

Inspecta Pro and AI credits are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card details. To know whether you have Pro, Inspecta uses RevenueCat, which receives a random user ID (or your Google account ID if you have signed in), the products you bought and when, and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: for Google Drive backups, AI photo reading, ads and checking Pro. Inspections, photos, signatures and reports all work without the internet.
  • Camera: to take inspection photos and read text from them. Photos are saved only in Inspecta.
  • Photos: Inspecta uses Android's photo picker, so it only receives the photos you choose.
  • Billing: to buy Pro and AI credits through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, as described above.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. The only services that receive information from Inspecta are the ones named in this policy: Google (Drive backups you choose, Firebase, AdMob and Play billing), OpenAI (only when you use AI photo reading) and RevenueCat (Pro). Some of these services process information outside South Africa, under their own safeguards. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long information is kept

  • Inspections on your phone: until you delete them or uninstall Inspecta.
  • Google Drive backups: until you delete them. Inspecta keeps your three newest Drive backups and removes older ones.
  • AI credit records: for as long as you use AI photo reading, then deleted on request.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its use. Because your inspections are on your phone, you can see, change and delete them yourself in Inspecta. For your AI credit records, email us and we will delete them.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Inspecta is a work app for adults. It is not meant for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Your inspections are kept in Inspecta's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Information sent for Drive backups and AI photo reading travels over encrypted connections. Keep your phone locked, and keep backup files somewhere safe.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and tell you in the app about important changes.
