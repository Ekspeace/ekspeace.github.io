Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or ID number.
  • Your progress, scores, hints and settings stay on your phone.
  • Data leaves your phone only through Google's advertising and Google Play's billing.
  • We don't sell your data or keep a profile of you.

Who we are

Quiz Mzansi ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Ekspeace. Contact us at Ekspeace@gmail.com.

Quiz Mzansi is a general quiz game. It is not directed at children under 13.

Logos and brand names shown in the quiz belong to their owners. Quiz Mzansi is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. Flag images come from the open-source flag-icons project (MIT licence).

Advertising

Quiz Mzansi is free and funded by ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device information and your advertising ID to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and a "Privacy options" entry in the app's settings lets you change your choice.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings under Google > Ads.

No ads are shown while a question is on screen. If you buy "remove ads", banners and ads between games are switched off. Rewarded videos stay available because you choose to watch them.

Purchases

The hint pack and the once-off "remove ads" item are bought through Google Play's billing system under Google's own terms and privacy policy. "Remove ads" is tied to the Google account on your phone, so it is restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. Hints are stored on your phone and are not restored after uninstalling. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers or collect analytics.
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with data brokers.

Deleting your data

Settings > Reset progress clears your solved levels and scores. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. For data held by Google (ads and purchases), use your Google account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
