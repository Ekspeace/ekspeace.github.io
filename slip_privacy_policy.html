Slip Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

In short

  • Your business records stay on your phone. Slip has no account to create and no server that stores your invoices, customers or expenses.
  • Backups to Google Drive go to your own Drive, in a private folder only Slip can use.
  • Slip AI is optional. When you use it, the slip photo or job description you choose is sent to be read, and is not kept by Slip.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Slip Pro has no ads.
  • We never sell your information.

Who we are

Slip is an app made by Ekspeace in South Africa ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Slip app for Android handles, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and Google Play's requirements.

Questions or requests: ekspeace@gmail.com. Our Information Officer can be reached at the same address.

Information that stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Slip is saved only in Slip's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • your company details, logo, signature and banking details;
  • customers and suppliers, including names, phone numbers, email and street addresses and VAT numbers;
  • quotes, invoices, receipts, statements, letters and other documents;
  • expenses, photos of slips, scans and your price list;
  • your settings.

We do not receive or see any of this. If you uninstall Slip, Android deletes it, so please make a backup first.

When you share a document, Slip hands it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or email. What happens next is up to that app and the person you send it to.

Backups

Backup files

A backup file contains your records, logos and, if you choose, your scans. It is not protected by a password, so keep it somewhere safe, such as your own cloud storage.

Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Slip asks Google for permission to use only its own hidden app folder in your Drive. Slip cannot see, open or change any of your other files. Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Slip so the app can show which account is connected. The backups belong to you, live in your Google account, and are covered by Google's privacy policy. We cannot access them.

To remove them, open Google Drive on a computer, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Slip and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can disconnect Drive in Slip at any time.

Slip AI (optional)

Slip AI only runs when you tap one of its buttons. Depending on what you use, it sends:

  • Fill in from a slip: the photo you choose, made smaller first;
  • Describe the job: the text you type, with the names and prices on your price list so it can use your prices.

This goes over an encrypted connection to Slip's server, run on Supabase, and from there to OpenAI, which reads it and sends the result back to your phone. Neither Slip's server nor we keep the photo, the text or the result. OpenAI processes it under its terms for business use of its API, which state that this information is not used to train its models by default and may be kept for a limited time to prevent abuse.

To count how many AI actions you have used each month, Slip creates a random ID for your phone the first time you use Slip AI. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number. Our server stores only this ID and a monthly count. If you have Slip Pro, the ID is also linked to your purchase so the server can give you the Pro allowance.

Please do not photograph or describe information you are not allowed to share with a service provider.

Advertising

The free version of Slip shows ads, including optional videos you can watch to unlock a feature, from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, device type and how you interact with ads, and may use it to show ads that suit your interests. This is covered by Google's advertising policies.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads, depending on your phone). Slip Pro removes ads.

Payments

Slip Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card details. To know whether you have Pro, Slip uses RevenueCat, which receives a random user ID, the products you bought and when, and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: for Google Drive backups, Slip AI, ads and checking Pro. Everything else works without the internet.
  • Camera and photos: Slip uses Android's own camera and photo picker, so it only ever receives the photos you take or choose.
  • Billing: to buy Slip Pro through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, as described above.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. The only services that receive information from Slip are the ones named in this policy: Google (Drive backups you choose, AdMob and Play billing), Supabase and OpenAI (only when you use Slip AI) and RevenueCat (Pro). Some of these services process information outside South Africa, under their own safeguards. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long information is kept

  • Records on your phone: until you delete them or uninstall Slip.
  • Google Drive backups: until you delete them. Slip keeps your three newest Drive backups and removes older ones.
  • Slip AI usage counts: for as long as you use Slip AI, then removed on request.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its use. Because your records are on your phone, you can see, change and delete them yourself in Slip. For the Slip AI usage count, email us and we will delete it.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Slip is a business app for adults. It is not meant for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Your records are kept in Slip's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Information sent for Drive backups and Slip AI travels over encrypted connections. Keep your phone locked, and keep backup files somewhere safe.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app about important changes.
