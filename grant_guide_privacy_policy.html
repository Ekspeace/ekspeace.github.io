Grant Guide SA: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 (news added)

Grant Guide SA is not a government app. It is not linked to, endorsed by or run by the South African Social Security Agency (SASSA) or any government department. It gives general information from official sources. Applications, status checks and payments happen only on SASSA's own channels.

Grant Guide SA ("the app") is published by Ekspeace. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

We never ask for your ID number

The app never asks for, collects or stores ID numbers, SASSA card numbers, bank details, PINs or passwords. If anyone asks you for these in the name of this app, it is a scam.

Information that stays on your phone

These are saved only on your device and are never sent to us: family members you add (a name you choose, the grant, and optional dates such as a child's birthday or a grant end date), document checklist ticks, your budget, your stokvel tracker, your language and text size. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Reminders and notifications

Payment and life-event reminders are scheduled on your phone and work without internet. News notifications are created on your phone after the background check above; we do not use push notification services or device tokens.

News

News posts are written by Ekspeace in our own words, with links to the original sources. They are not official SASSA notices.

Links to official websites

Buttons such as "Open the SRD website" open the official website in your browser. What you enter there is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is meant for adults who receive or help others apply for social grants. It is not directed at children.

Your rights (POPIA)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act you may ask what personal information we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Because the app does not ask for your name or contact details, we usually hold none that identifies you. Queue reports can be removed on request.
