KASI CRUNCH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or ID number.
  • Your progress, stars, boosters, lives and settings stay on your phone, unless you choose to save your progress to Google Play Games.
  • Data leaves your phone only through Google's advertising, Google Play's billing and, if you sign in, Google Play Games.
  • We don't sell your data or keep a profile of you.

Who we are

Kasi Crunch ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Ekspeace. Contact us at Ekspeace@gmail.com.

Kasi Crunch is a puzzle game for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13.

Advertising

Kasi Crunch is free to play and funded by ads from Google AdMob. A rewarded video only plays when you choose to watch one. Now and then, after you win a level, a full-screen ad shows before the next screen: at most every second win, never in the first levels, never after a loss and never with Kasi Crunch Pro. There are no banner ads. Google may collect and use device information and your advertising ID to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and a "Privacy choices" entry in the app's settings lets you change your choice.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings under Google > Ads.

Cloud save

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. If you do, the app saves your levels, stars, best scores, boosters and which explainers you have seen to Google Play Games saved games in your Google account, and reads them back on another phone. Lives and settings are not saved there. Google Play Games is run by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.

Purchases

The once-off "Kasi Crunch Pro" purchase is made through Google Play's billing system under Google's own terms and privacy policy. It is tied to the Google account on your phone, so it is restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers for this app and hold no database of players.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • There is no chat and no way to contact other players inside the app.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. To delete it, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data held by Google (advertising, purchases and Google Play Games saved games) is managed in your Google account. A cloud save can be deleted in the Google Play Games settings.

Children

Kasi Crunch is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you have a concern, contact us at Ekspeace@gmail.com.

Your rights

In South Africa the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you the right to know what personal information is held about you and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. If you have a concern, contact us first at Ekspeace@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date at the top.
