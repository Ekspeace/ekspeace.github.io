APS ACE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

In short

  • Your marks stay on your phone. Working out your APS, your pass level and what you qualify for happens entirely on your device.
  • We have no accounts and ask for no name, email, ID number or phone number.
  • Some data leaves your phone only when you use an online extra (Ask Coach, the motivation letter helper), and through Google's advertising.
  • We don't sell your data or keep a profile of you.

Who we are

APS Ace ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Ekspeace. Contact us at Ekspeace@gmail.com.

APS Ace is an independent guide. It is not affiliated with any university, TVET college, the Department of Basic Education or the Department of Higher Education and Training.

What data is used, and why

DataWhere it goesWhy
Your subject marks, shortlist and app settings Stored only on your device To calculate your APS, pass level and matches
Your question to Coach, plus your marks, APS, pass level and a list of programmes you match Our secure backend (Supabase), then OpenAI To answer your question about your options
What you type into the motivation letter helper (and your APS and pass level, only if you switch that on) Our secure backend (Supabase), then OpenAI To write a first draft of your letter
Advertising ID, device and app-usage information Google AdMob To show ads, including rewarded ads that unlock extras, and to prevent ad fraud
App version and store information Google Play or the Apple App Store To tell you when a new version of the app is available

Ask Coach and the motivation letter helper

These optional tools need an internet connection. When you use them, the app sends only what is listed above to our backend on Supabase, which passes it to our AI provider, OpenAI, to generate a reply.

  • We do not send your name, contact details or device identifiers with these requests.
  • We do not store your questions, letters or replies, and do not use them to build a profile of you.
  • Please don't type personal details such as your ID number, address or phone number into these tools.
  • AI replies can be wrong. Always confirm requirements and closing dates with the institution.

Grade 12 past exam papers

Past papers open from the Department of Basic Education's website (education.gov.za) in your browser. That website's own privacy practices apply when you download from it.

Advertising

APS Ace is free and funded by ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device information and your advertising ID to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and an "Ad privacy options" button lets you change your choice at any time.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Young users

APS Ace is made for Grade 11 and Grade 12 learners, their parents and teachers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we ask advertisers' networks to show only age-appropriate ads.

Because many learners are under 18, we collect as little as possible: no accounts, no names, and marks that stay on the phone. Learners under 18 should use the online AI tools with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your device stays until you clear it in the app ("Clear" on the marks screen), clear the app's data, or uninstall.
  • Coach and letter requests are processed to produce a reply and not kept by us. OpenAI and Supabase may keep request logs for a limited time under their own policies.
  • All data sent over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and laws such as the GDPR where they apply, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Because we hold no accounts or profiles, most requests are handled by clearing the app's data, but you can always contact Ekspeace@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
