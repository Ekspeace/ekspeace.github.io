Privacy policy: Matric Ace

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Matric Ace is a study app for CAPS Mathematics and Physical Sciences. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: we collect nothing about you ourselves, your work stays on your phone, and the only outside party is Google AdMob, which supplies the adverts.

Information we collect

Matric Ace has no account, no sign-in and no analytics of our own. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we never see your answers or your progress.

The app does show adverts, supplied by Google AdMob, and that is the one part that uses the internet. To choose and measure adverts, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about the advert you were shown. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Before any advert loads, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it, using Google's consent tool. You can change that choice later from the privacy options the tool provides.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves your progress on the device itself, so it can show your daily goal, your streak and which questions you have already seen. That includes:

  • the grade you selected and the daily goal you chose
  • how many questions you answered on each day
  • which questions you have seen, and which you scored below half on
  • whether you turned the daily reminder on, and the time you chose
  • any school exam date you added, and your text size setting

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only to fetch adverts. The question bank, marking and your progress are all on the device, so the app works with no connection.
  • Notifications: only to show the daily practice reminder, and only if you turn the reminder on. You can turn it off in the app at any time, or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Run at start-up: so that a reminder you have already set survives a phone restart. It is used for nothing else.
  • Vibrate: used by the notification when your phone is set to vibrate.

Children

Matric Ace is aimed at high school learners, including learners under 18. Because of that, the adverts are requested as non-personalised for users in that age group, and the app uses Google's families-compatible advert settings. We collect nothing about a child ourselves.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell anything about you, and we hold nothing about you ourselves. The only third party in the app is Google AdMob, which serves the adverts and processes the advert data described above. There are no other trackers and no analytics libraries.

Changes to this policy
