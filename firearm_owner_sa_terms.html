1. What the app is

Firearm Owner SA is an independent study aid for people working towards a firearm competency certificate and a firearm licence in South Africa. It contains practice questions written from the published Firearms Control Act 60 of 2000 and from the assessment criteria registered with SAQA for the relevant unit standards, together with a walkthrough of the ownership process and a reference section.

3. Safe handling — read this

The app contains questions about safe handling, loading and unloading, malfunctions, range procedure and the storage of firearms, ammunition and black powder. That material exists to help you prepare for a written paper.

Never learn to handle a firearm from an app. Practical firearm handling must be taught and supervised in person by an accredited instructor. Where this app and your instructor, your range’s rules, or the firearm manufacturer’s instructions differ, follow the instructor, the range or the manufacturer — not the app.

Explosives, propellants and percussion caps are separately regulated and dangerous. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority before acquiring, storing or transporting them.

4. Accuracy, and the limits of it

We take accuracy seriously. Every answer names the provision or criterion it comes from so that you can check it against the source yourself, and the underlying material is public and free.

Even so, legislation changes, unit standards are re-registered or replaced, and administrative practice varies between police stations and providers. The app may contain errors or may be out of date. It is your responsibility to confirm anything that matters against the Act itself, the registered unit standard, your training provider, or the relevant authority.

If you find something wrong, please tell us at Ekspeace@gmail.com. Corrections are the most useful thing you can send.
