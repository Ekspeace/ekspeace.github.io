1. What IAC Ace is

IAC Ace is a spaced-repetition revision aid for candidates preparing for the SAICA Initial Assessment of Competence (IAC), formerly the Initial Test of Competence (ITC). It drills recall of definitions, criteria, thresholds, formulae and decision matrices, and provides a searchable reference to the published examinable scope.

It is a companion to a board course, not a replacement for one, and it is deliberately not a mock examination. The IAC consists of three long-form papers of integrated case study; nothing in this app simulates that.

4. Accuracy of content, and the year of assessment

The card bank is a seed set that has not been independently reviewed. Cards marked CHECK SOURCE state an interpretation rather than a figure printed in a public document, and should be checked against your own course material before you rely on them.

Tax figures expire, and the examinable year lags the current one. The IAC examines a specific year of assessment set by SAICA's Taxation Knowledge List, which is normally behind the figures currently published by SARS. The app states the year it is scoped to. Do not assume a figure in this app is the current SARS figure, and do not assume the current SARS figure is the examinable one.

The examinable scope shown in the app is our reading of SAICA's published scoping documents, extracted mechanically. It may contain errors or omissions. Always confirm against the current published documents before relying on a scope conclusion.
